Keyboard = Xbox pad: WASD left stick, arrows right stick, Space A, E B, Shift X, F Y, Z/C triggers, Q/R bumpers, Enter Start. Gamepads work directly.
Freestyle MX bike: F9 spawns it, E gets on/off, C throttle, Z brake, V clutch, hold Q/R + arrows for tricks.
Phones/tablets: on-screen pad (Bike = F9, Y gets on, RT throttle, hold LB/RB + right stick for tricks).
